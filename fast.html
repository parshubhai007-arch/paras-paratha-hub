About Us

Welcome to Paras Paratha Hub – the ultimate destination for authentic, homemade-style parathas. Founded in 2020 by Paras, our hub is built on a simple idea: serve hot, fresh, and affordable parathas that remind you of home.

Every paratha is prepared with love, using fresh ingredients and traditional recipes. Whether it’s the classic Aloo Paratha, the rich Paneer Paratha, or the flavorful Gobhi Paratha, we make sure each bite brings comfort and taste.

At Paras Paratha Hub, quality and speed go hand in hand. We believe good food should be accessible to everyone – that’s why our prices are kept pocket-friendly without compromising on taste.

So, come join us and experience the warmth of homemade parathas, served with a smile.
